Air-Gap Architecture

Nobody keeps their master vault on a burner phone. Tuck the rooted device behind the host as a silent USB bridge: type from Your PC, Your Phone, or Your Tablet over local Wi-Fi, and watch it punch straight into the wire.

01

Keep your daily driver stock

No need to root your primary phone or install software on your work PC. Your everyday device stays 100% stock, encrypted, and MDM-compliant. Only the dedicated bridge phone handles the physical USB gadget.

02

Direct from your password vault

Keep 1Password, Bitwarden, or your password vault right where they belong on Your PC. Copy complex 64-character passphrases and paste them straight into Emittr's web interface. They land on the locked PC in milliseconds.

03

100% offline & air-gap safe

Zero cloud servers, zero internet access needed. Emittr runs a direct local HTTP and WebSocket server. Connect through office Wi-Fi, a closed subnet switch, or directly to the phone's built-in Wi-Fi hotspot in the field.

Under the glass

Nine bytes.
That's the whole secret.

A keyboard report is nine bytes long. Your phone writes them to /dev/hidg0: and any computer, at any stage of boot, already knows exactly what to do with them.

Six more for the mouse, and the last one is why horizontal scrolling just works. Real USB Consumer usage 0x0238, no modifier-key workarounds.

Install

Twominutes.

Clone it, run the installer, plug in. The daemon, the gadget and the web deck all come up on their own.

Needs: a rooted Android phone with USB gadget (ConfigFS) support: most kernels from the last decade have it, and a data cable.

root@android
# 1 · install
$ git clone https://github.com/mr-anjaneyam/Emittr.git
$ cd Emittr && bash install.sh
 
# 2 · survive reboots (Magisk)
$ cp /opt/usb_hid_deck/02-usb-deck.sh /data/adb/service.d/
$ chmod +x /data/adb/service.d/02-usb-deck.sh
 
# 3 · plug in. open the deck.
→ http://localhost:8088
